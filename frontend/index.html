New to this? Plain-English glossary
Batch
One production lot of a medicine, like one tray of identical tablets from the same factory run.
Hand-over
One record saying a batch moved from one company to another.
Seal (digital fingerprint, called a hash)
A short code worked out from a record. Change even one digit of the record and the seal changes completely.
Chain
Each record also contains the seal of the step before it, so secretly editing an old record breaks every later link.
Saved records
Once a hand-over, reading or dispensing record is saved, nobody (not even the administrator) can edit or delete it. Any attempt is blocked, logged and shown to everyone.
Cold chain
Keeping medicines such as insulin and vaccines within a safe temperature range from factory to patient.
